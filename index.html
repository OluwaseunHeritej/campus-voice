    How Campus Voice works

    Students raise concerns and vote on them. At the end of every cycle, management commits to the most-voted concern and posts updates until it is resolved.

    1. Raise or upvote

      Sign in with your school email. Before posting, the site shows similar concerns so you can add your vote instead of repeating them. Each student gets a small number of posts per day.

    2. Votes decide

      One student, one vote per concern. You can take your vote back while voting is open.

    3. Cycle closes

      When the cycle ends (monthly or per semester), the top-voted open concern is picked automatically. Management cannot choose a different one.

    4. Follow the action

      Management posts updates on the committed concern: in progress, resolved, or declined with a reason. Everyone can see the full history.

    Anonymity

    Signing in proves you are a student. It does not label what you write.

    • No author on posts. A concern is saved with its title, details, category and date. There is no column for who wrote it.
    • No timestamps on posts. Only the day is saved, and post IDs are random, so a post cannot be matched to the moment someone signed in.
    • Votes use a one-way code. To stop double voting, each vote is stored with a scrambled code made from your account and a secret key. The key is locked away from the website and the management dashboard, so votes cannot be traced back to a student.
    • Management has no special view. The management dashboard shows the same posts students see, plus tools to post updates and hide posts that break the rules.

    Keep your own post anonymous too: avoid details that identify you, and do not name individual staff or students. Posts that do may be hidden.
